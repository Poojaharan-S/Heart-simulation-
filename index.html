3. Important Parts of the Heart

Superior vena cava

Carries low-oxygen blood from the upper body to the right atrium.

Inferior vena cava

Carries low-oxygen blood from the lower body to the right atrium.

Right atrium

Receives low-oxygen blood returning from the body.

Right ventricle

Pumps low-oxygen blood to the lungs through the pulmonary artery.

Pulmonary artery

Carries low-oxygen blood from the right ventricle to the lungs.

Pulmonary veins

Carry oxygen-rich blood from the lungs to the left atrium.

Left atrium

Receives oxygen-rich blood returning from the lungs.

Left ventricle

Pumps oxygen-rich blood into the aorta and out to the body.

Aorta

The main artery carrying oxygen-rich blood from the left ventricle to the body.

Septum

The wall that separates the right and left sides of the heart.

Heart valves

Help keep blood flowing in the correct direction through the heart.

Capillaries

Tiny blood vessels where oxygen and other substances are exchanged with body tissues.

4. How Blood Circulates

  1. Blood low in oxygen returns from the body through the superior and inferior venae cavae to the right atrium.
  2. Blood passes through the tricuspid valve into the right ventricle.
  3. The right ventricle pumps blood through the pulmonary valve and pulmonary artery to the lungs.
  4. In the lungs, blood releases carbon dioxide and receives oxygen.
  5. Oxygen-rich blood returns through the pulmonary veins to the left atrium.
  6. Blood passes through the mitral valve into the left ventricle.
  7. The left ventricle pumps blood through the aortic valve and aorta to the rest of the body.

5. Did You Know?

  • The human heart has four chambers.
  • The right side sends blood to the lungs.
  • The left side sends blood to the rest of the body.
  • Heart valves help prevent blood from flowing backward.
  • The heart is made of specialized cardiac muscle.
