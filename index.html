Blood Circulation

🔵 Oxygen-Poor Blood Body → Venae cavae → Right atrium → Right ventricle → Pulmonary artery → Lungs.
🔴 Oxygen-Rich Blood Lungs → Pulmonary veins → Left atrium → Left ventricle → Aorta → Body.
